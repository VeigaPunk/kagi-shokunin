## Three most important things from the last 3 days

### 1. The op newline contract is now measured in bytes, not lines (depth)

`aea7a43` adds an op-only `--dotenv-bytes` arm that feeds six generated env files
(escaped LF, trailing LF, newline-only, single-quoted literal `\n`, double- and
single-quoted multiline) through `op run --env-file` and compares the child's
output as hex against literal measured bytes. The lesson behind it is recorded in
`corpus/lessons/round-20.md`: the old `collect()` path used shell command
substitution, which silently drops trailing LFs (`alpha\n\n` came back as
`61 6c 70 68 61`). Hex-encoding before anything enters `$( )` closes that
representation gap. The arm also runs `op` under `env -i` with an isolated
HOME/XDG tree, biometric unlock off and a 10 s timeout, so the measurement can't
leak into or borrow from the real session, and it refuses `--fixture` so it never
touches the real `op.env`. Seven selftest cases prove the comparator catches lost
trailing LFs, lost newline-only values, exit mismatches, missing rows and extra
rows. Round 20 recorded `op --version` 2.40.0 and `OP_DOTENV_BYTES: CLEAN
(checks=6 failed=0)`.
