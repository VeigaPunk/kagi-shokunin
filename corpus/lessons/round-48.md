## What the trial taught (beyond the five tests)

- The data key is not visible in the file: the per-recipient `enc` blocks change on every save even when the data key does not (K48ReviewerTrial K48RT-01), so "did the data key rotate?" is answered by value ciphertexts and the MAC, or by unwrapping and hashing the key — never by diffing `enc`.
- Removing a whole key group with `updatekeys` needs the old threshold's identities on the syncing host; with only the remaining group present it exits 128 and leaves the removed key's access intact (K48CriticCeiling B1). The compromise order in the entry is now qualified by that measurement.
- sops 3.13.3 refuses `--shamir-secret-sharing-threshold 1` over two groups ("threshold must be at least 2"), so the designed T5 falsifier had to collapse the groups instead (counter SH5 comment).
